The Scarlet Letter F: a manuscript-to-learning bridge

The developing manuscript frames the problem of felony stigma and persistent barriers to participation. The Academy translates that inquiry into digital literacy, critical AI-use skills and entrepreneurship training.

BARRIER

Stigma & structural obstacles

Employment, licensing, financing and other opportunities can be affected by criminal records; impacts vary by jurisdiction and circumstance.

BARRIER

The digital divide

Long incarceration may limit exposure to modern devices, cloud software, AI tools and digital business workflows.

RESPONSE

Capability & agency

Build practical digital competence, evidence-based judgment, business literacy and documented work products.

Two-stage delivery model

STAGE 1

Pre-release education

Instructor-led lessons, offline-compatible content, printed exercises, simulated model comparisons and controlled assessments.

STAGE 2

Post-release application

Supervised live AI access, speech-to-text, audio review, multi-model research, entrepreneurship labs and portfolio coaching.

Institution-specific rules govern internet access, recordings, devices and external services. The prototype does not assume live AI access inside correctional facilities.

Practical entrepreneurial outcomes

01

Identify a viable problem

Customer interviews, needs analysis, competitor research and ethical market validation.

02

Build an operating plan

Registration and licensing research, pricing, basic financials, compliance and marketing.

03

Demonstrate independent judgment

A verified research portfolio with TTL decisions and a defendable business concept.

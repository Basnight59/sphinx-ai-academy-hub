Foundational framework

The Sphinx Method

Human-directed, multimodal, multi-LLM research and learning governed by TTL—Truth, Transparency, and Legacy.

Test the principles

Three pillars, one accountable process

T

Truth

Interrogate claims; seek primary evidence; distinguish uncertainty from verified findings.

T

Transparency

Preserve the questions, model responses, objections, corrections and human decisions.

L

Legacy

Retain an intelligible record of how understanding and conclusions developed.

GIGO: a spectrum, not a verdict

Garbage In, Garbage Out ↔ Gold In, Gold Out. Subject expertise, experience, question quality, evidence and critical judgment influence results. Formal credentials alone do not determine quality.

LOW-QUALITY CONDITIONS

Garbage tendency

Vague requests, unsupported assumptions, unexamined output and missing verification.

HIGH-QUALITY CONDITIONS

Gold tendency

Clear context, competing hypotheses, checked sources, explicit uncertainty and accountable adjudication.

The spectrum is conceptual. Better inputs improve the conditions for better outcomes; they do not guarantee correctness.

The human intellectual engagement loop

Frame the question

Draw on lived experience, occupational knowledge or formal study.

Interrogate multiple systems

Use typed prompts, spoken dialogue and independent model responses.

Read and listen

Examine wording, hear the argument and revise the inquiry.

Verify and challenge

Check primary sources and investigate disagreements.

Adjudicate and preserve

Document the human decision and archive the intellectual record.

Important distinction

Agentic systems can support logging, audio, human review and evidence workflows. The Sphinx distinction is the deliberately sustained human intellectual practice and its governance, not a claim that APIs are technically incapable of these functions.

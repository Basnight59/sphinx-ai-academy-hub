The learning architecture

A proposed modular curriculum that moves from digital foundations to critical AI use, human adjudication, entrepreneurship and an applied capstone.

Practice in the Lab
Ten-module pathway

Learn, demonstrate, document

MODULE 01

Digital Foundations

Devices, online accounts, privacy, cybersecurity and information access.

MODULE 02

AI Literacy

LLM capabilities, limitations, hallucinations and responsible use.

MODULE 03

GIGO Quality Principle

Garbage In / Garbage Out and Gold In / Gold Out as a teachable quality spectrum.

MODULE 04

Manual Orchestration

Deliberate cross-platform questioning and model comparison.

MODULE 05

Multimodal Review

Speech-to-text, audio playback, reading and iterative revision.

MODULE 06

Adversarial Analysis

Conflicting outputs, source checks and alternative interpretations.

MODULE 07

TTL: Truth

Evidence verification and responsible conclusions.

MODULE 08

TTL: Transparency & Legacy

Research logs, human adjudication, version history and preservation.

MODULE 09

Entrepreneurship

Customer discovery, business models, pricing and practical planning.

MODULE 10

Applied Capstone

A human-reviewed research or business artifact with a documented evidence trail.

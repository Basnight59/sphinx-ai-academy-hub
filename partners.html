Proposed implementation sequence

Discovery and constraints

Identify learner needs, facility rules, literacy levels, accessibility requirements, equipment and LMS compatibility.

Baseline assessment

Measure digital literacy, research practices, verification skills and learner confidence without treating credentials as proxies for ability.

Deliver a bounded pilot

Use SCORM-compatible modules where appropriate, instructor facilitation and practical learner artifacts.

Human-reviewed evaluation

Assess reasoning, evidence quality, error detection and transfer to real tasks, not only completion rates.

Refine and scale

Use pilot evidence to revise materials, accessibility, coaching and post-release continuity.

Delivery and governance requirements

LEARNING TECHNOLOGY

LMS / SCORM readiness

Select SCORM version, completion criteria, reporting needs, offline constraints and accessibility tests.

LEARNER SAFETY

Data & privacy

Protect sensitive personal and correctional information; obtain consent and define data retention and access.

EDUCATIONAL VALIDITY

Assessment quality

Use pre/post measures, rubrics, learner artifacts and human review to test whether the method improves capability.

INSTITUTIONAL FIT

Pathway continuity

Connect pre-release education with community-based support, workforce services and entrepreneurship mentoring.
